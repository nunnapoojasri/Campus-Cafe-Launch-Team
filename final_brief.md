# Decision brief: A measured launch for a Campus Café

**Recommendation:** Approve a short, conditional pilot-planning phase—not a full operating-budget commitment—while the university validates student demand, campus pricing, and a staffed operating plan.

**Why explore it.** The National Coffee Association's 2026 National Coffee Data Trends report says 47% of U.S. adults had specialty coffee in the past day, compared with 42% for traditional coffee; 38% of past-day coffee drinkers had coffee out of home, the highest share since January 2020. This is useful category context, not a forecast for our students. The Hope Center's Student Basic Needs Survey is designed to help institutions document needs in their own student populations. Use a short campus survey and purchase-intent test to learn what students will actually buy and at what price. [NCA, 2026](https://www.ncausa.org/Newsroom/Coffee-Consumption-Statistics) · [Hope Center survey](https://hope.temple.edu/research/hope-center-basic-needs-survey)

**Offer and pricing to test.** Test accessible brewed coffee, a small range of specialty drinks, affordable snacks, and one plant-based option. Illustrative menu targets—$3.25 brewed coffee, $5.25 specialty drink, and $3.75 snack—are planning assumptions, not sourced national or local price benchmarks; compare nearby menus and test willingness to pay before setting prices. Sustainability practices—such as reusable-cup incentives and tracking unsold food—are pilot hypotheses, not proven local preferences. IFIC's 2025 Food & Health Survey examines consumer food and purchasing decisions, but does not establish demand on this campus. The $7.50 average transaction below is a separate blended-ticket assumption, not calculated from those target prices. [IFIC, 2025 survey](https://ific.org/research/2025-food-health-survey/)

**Illustrative monthly operating break-even** *(all inputs below are planning assumptions)*:

| Assumption | Monthly amount |
|---|---:|
| Rent, occupancy, utilities | $4,000 |
| Staffing | $12,500 |
| Equipment lease/maintenance | $1,500 |
| Other operating costs | $2,000 |
| **Fixed costs** | **$20,000** |
| Average transaction | $7.50 |
| Variable costs (ingredients, packaging, fees) | 35% of sales |
| Open days | 22 |

**Arithmetic:** contribution per transaction = $7.50 × (1 − 0.35) = **$4.875**. Monthly break-even transactions = $20,000 ÷ $4.875 = **4,103**. Daily break-even = 4,103 ÷ 22 = **about 187 transactions per open day**. That equals transactions of about 2.3% of the assumed 8,000-student enrollment per open day; it is not the share of unique students, since repeat visits are possible. This is an operating estimate, not a sales forecast. It excludes start-up fit-out, financing, taxes, and seasonal swings; labor and fixed-cost estimates need Facilities and HR quotes.

**Decision requested:** authorize a limited validation phase: survey students on price and menu preferences, count foot traffic at candidate locations, obtain supplier/labor/facilities quotes, and return with a revised downside/base/upside case before approving launch funds.
